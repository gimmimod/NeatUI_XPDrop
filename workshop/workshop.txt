description=[h1]⚡ NeatUI XP Drop[/h1]
description=A standalone XP bar for Build 42, styled to match NeatUI. Rounded panels, orange accent, a live progress bar and a clean config window. 🧡
description=
description=[h2]✨ What it does[/h2]
description=Every time you earn XP the panel switches to that skill and floats the amount above itself with the skill icon. Level, progress bar and the exact readout stay on screen, so you always know how close the next level is.
description=[list]
description=[*] 🎯 Floating XP numbers with the skill icon
description=[*] 📊 Level, progress bar and XP readout at a glance
description=[*] 💬 Hover for the breakdown: current, next level, remaining, progress
description=[*] 🗂️ Skills grouped by category — Combat, Firearms, Crafting, Survivalist, Physical, Farming
description=[*] 🧩 Skills added by other mods get their own section in the settings, so you can track them too
description=[*] 🎨 Bundled OSRS-style icon set, with a drawn badge for anything missing
description=[*] 🌍 Localized in all official game languages
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🖱️ Controls[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Drag[/b] the panel to move it
description=[*] [b]Right-click[/b] to open the settings, and right-click a skill in there for its breakdown
description=[*] [b]Drag the corner grip[/b], bottom right, to resize
description=[*] [b]Hover[/b] for the detailed tooltip
description=[*] Hidden, it collapses to a small badge — click it to bring the panel back
description=[*] Optional key binding under [i][NeatUI XP Drop][/i] in Options → Key Bindings
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]⚙️ Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Right-click on the panel[/b]: switch individual skills on or off, click a category title to toggle a whole group, All / None, the lock that pins size and position, and the button that hides the panel.
description=[b]Options → Mods → NeatUI XP Drop[/b]: auto-hide, stacked panels and which side they stack on, whether the numbers rise above the panel or fall below it, numbers over your character, replacing the game's own notices, colour pickers for the bar gradient and the panel text, reset position, and hide the mod completely.
description=
description=[h2]🧭 Where the numbers go[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] 🪜 [b]Stacked panels[/b] — when several skills gain XP at once they stop overwriting each other: up to three panels appear, one below the other, and each number rises over its own skill
description=[*] 🧍 [b]Over your character[/b] — the numbers follow you around the world instead of sitting on the panel, with the skill icon. They keep working with the panel folded away
description=[*] ↕️ [b]Up or down, either way[/b] — the numbers can fall below the panel instead of rising above it, and the stacked panels can go above the main one instead of below. Pick the pair that suits where you keep the panel; the numbers never cross a panel whichever you choose
description=[*] 📈 [b]XP rate[/b] — hover the panel and the last line tells you how fast that skill is learning, with an arrow when a multiplier is running
description=[*] 🔁 [b]One look for everything[/b] — on by default, what radio and TV write above your head comes back in ours: Neat arrow and lettering, with the skill icon when it is a skill. XP, boredom, unhappiness and the rest, in one style instead of two. The arrow points where the game pointed it, green for good news and red for bad. The game's crafting XP text goes quiet at the same time
description=[/list]
